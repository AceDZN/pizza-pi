# Pizza Pi — curriculum links

Source of truth: Workbook (math.workbook.co.il). Every route below was read through the Workbook MCP or from the saved snapshots in `reports/math-games-2026-10-24/curriculum/` of the Idea Plotter repo. The circle modules are grade-7 prerequisites that grade 8 applies; the game uses them inside grade-8 percent, ratio and similarity work.

| Level | What the player does | Skill practiced | Workbook module |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · הפיצה הראשונה | Order says **קוטר 20**; the ruler measures **רדיוס**. Stretch r = 10, squeeze ≈ 314 cm² of sauce. r = 20 makes a tray. | d = 2r; disk area πr² | [היקף מעגל והמספר פאי](https://math.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-8/circle-circumference) · [שטח עיגול](https://math.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-8/circle-area) |
| 2 · קרום ממולא | r = 15; cut a cheese rope of 2πr ≈ 94.2 cm (±4%). Short leaves a gap that leaks in the oven; long makes a lump. | circumference 2πr vs πr vs πr² | [היקף מעגל והמספר פאי](https://math.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-8/circle-circumference) |
| 3 · שלושה ילדים רעבים | 3 × 200 cm² ⇒ smallest r on the 0.5 cm grid with πr² ≥ 600 is 14 (615.8). 13.5 (572.6) is too small; bigger wastes dough. | inverse use of the area formula, inequality, rounding up | [שטח עיגול](https://math.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-8/circle-area) |

Terms used in the UI follow Workbook: רדיוס, קוטר, היקף, שטח, סמ״ר, ס״מ, יחס, מקדם כיפלי, אחוז שינוי. π is `Math.PI` in the rules; the notebook and calculator show 3.14 / π, which is inside every tolerance.

Tolerances: sauce ±5% of πr² for the pie actually stretched; crust ±4% of 2πr; dough ratio ±3%. Dough size is exact on the 0.5 cm grid (the order fixes it).

Not covered here (by design of the three-game split): קנה מידה (the reserve game Treasure Charts), Pythagoras, linear equations. Optional later content from the handoff that is not built: a 15%-off coupon on the price board and a deep-dish level using πr²h ([נפח גליל](https://math.workbook.co.il/grade-8/chapter-9/cylinder-volume)).
